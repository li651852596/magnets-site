About us

KYHS — your direct line to a custom fridge magnets factory

We are the fridge-magnet division of KEYUE HONGSHENG I/E CO., LTD., focused on custom magnetic souvenirs for retailers, brands and gift buyers around the world.

What we stand for

We started with one simple belief: custom magnets should be affordable, high-quality and easy to order — even for small businesses. By running our own production and keeping the supply chain short, we pass the savings on to you and keep full control of quality.

From a single souvenir design to a full retail program, our team supports your project with design proofs, material advice and careful inspection before every shipment.

Our custom fridge magnets come in wooden, metal, resin, epoxy and PVC finishes, with magnetic sheets, rigid backing or flexible vinyl options. We produce shaped cuts, full-colour prints, embossed logos and glow-in-the-dark effects, and we can match Pantone colours for brand programmes. Whether you need magnetic fridge magnets for a museum gift shop, a hotel souvenir line or a promotional campaign, we advise on the material and finish that best fits your budget and quantity.

Low minimum order quantities make it practical to test a new design, and our in-house artwork team turns your logo or sketch into a production-ready proof within a day. As a factory-direct supplier, we control each step from mould making to packing, so quality stays consistent across repeat orders.
